My Story

It started with my own hair.

5+Years of experience
2022Lopezcuts founded
250+SLC clients in 1.5 years

I've been cutting hair for more than 5 years, and the first head I ever cut was my own. I didn't have a plan. I had a mirror, some patience, and a feeling that this was something I could be great at.

Then my friends started asking. Those first cuts were for supportive friends who trusted me before I'd really earned it. Word spread, and one chair turned into hundreds of new, friendly faces I'm lucky enough to call clients.

It wasn't easy. Building a real business in high school meant splitting my life between two different places, stretching every dollar to cover tools and expenses, and balancing school with time to just be a kid. Plenty of times it would've been easier to quit. I never wanted to.

Then came the hardest part: leaving the DC/Virginia area for the University of Utah. Saying goodbye to the clients who built me up and starting over from zero in a brand-new city was one of the toughest things I've done. But I kept showing up, one cut at a time, and in about a year and a half here in Salt Lake City, Lopezcuts has grown to more than 250 clients.

This isn't a side hustle or a phase. It's my passion, and it's what I want to do for the rest of my life.

“Nothing beats the moment someone looks in the mirror and lights up. Making people look good, feel good, and walk out more confident than they walked in is why I do this. I love my job and everything about it.”

Miguel LopezFounder & Barber

Questions

Good to know.

How much is a haircut?

$25 for your first cut and $30 after that. Add a beard or design for +$5. Touch-ups are free.

How long does a cut take?

Up to 40 minutes, so you can fit it between classes or before work.

Where are you located?

Salt Lake City, Utah. The exact address is sent with your confirmation text so it stays private.

Do I need an app or an account?

No. Book right on this site in about a minute with your name and phone number.

How far ahead can I book?

Up to 3 months ahead, and as late as 12 hours before an open time.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Use your booking link up to 6 hours before your appointment. Closer than that, just reply to Miguel’s text.

What if something’s off after my cut?

Reach out and come back in. Touch-ups are free, especially for curly hair.

What styles do you do?

Taper fades, burst fades, modern and burst mullets, mod cuts, scissor cuts, lineups, curly hair and beards. Bring a photo if you have one.

How do I pay?

You pay after your cut. Zelle is preferred, and cash, Venmo and Apple Pay all work too.
