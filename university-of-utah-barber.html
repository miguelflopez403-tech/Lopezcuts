Questions

Quick answers

How much is a haircut near the U?

$25 for your first cut and $30 after that. Add a beard or design for +$5. Small touch-ups after your cut are free.

Do you have any spots open this week?

Live openings are on lopezcuts.com. Booking takes about a minute, with no app or account. If a time shows up there, it's yours.

Where are you located?

Salt Lake City, close to the University of Utah campus. Once you book, Miguel texts you the exact address the morning of your appointment.

Do you cut curly hair?

Yes. Curly hair is some of Miguel's favorite work, and if a curl settles differently after a few days, the touch-up is free.

How long does a cut take?

Up to 40 minutes, so it fits between classes.
