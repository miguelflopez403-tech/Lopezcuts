My routine

How I style it.

  1. 1

    Start damp

    Right after a shower, put in your sea salt spray and/or the leave-in.

  2. 2

    Dry it a bit

    Blow dry or diffuse, and stop just before it's all the way dry.

  3. 3

    Nickel of paste

    Take a nickel-sized amount and spread it evenly through your hands and fingers.

  4. 4

    Work it in gently

    Mix it in from the top. Be gentle. Don't scrunch and don't scratch.

  5. 5

    Quick dry

    Dry it up real quick one more time.

  6. 6

    Seal it

    Add just a bit of styling powder for the final seal and hold, so it doesn't move all day.
