About Miguel

From the DMV to Salt Lake City.

Miguel Lopez is a barber in Salt Lake City and the founder of Lopez Cuts (also written Lopezcuts). He picked up clippers at 14, practiced on his own hair, and started cutting for friends in the DC/Virginia area. Word spread, and in high school he was already running a real barber business, splitting time between two places and covering his own tools and expenses.

When he moved to Salt Lake City for the University of Utah, he started over from zero. One cut at a time, Lopez Cuts grew to more than 250 clients in about a year and a half, with a 5.0 rating from 34 reviews.

Miguel specializes in taper fades, burst fades, skin fades, modern and burst mullets, mod cuts, scissor work, lineups, curly hair, beards and designs. Cuts take up to 40 minutes, a first cut is $25, and touch-ups after an appointment are free.

He's also looking into cosmetology so Lopez Cuts can offer women's hair, and a Lopez Cuts app is in the works. When he's back home in the DMV, he takes Hometown Haircut bookings for clients there.

“This isn't a side hustle or a phase. It's my passion.”
