How it started

Upstairs at my mom's house.

Me and two of my buddies were hanging out in my room when my boy Carlos asked me to cut just the sides of his head. We messed around with the clippers, laughing, half joking that one day it would be "Lopez Cuts" and telling me I should really start cutting hair.

Then it stopped being a joke. One by one, more friends sat down in that chair and let me practice on them. That trust is still the whole business.

"They put their trust in me knowing it would pay off one day."
